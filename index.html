My role: Solo designer Coimbatore, TN

Kaapi Kadai: brand identity for a filter-coffee shop

What I did

Designed the logo, a bilingual Tamil–English menu board, cup sleeves and an Instagram post template for a small coffee shop near my college. I sketched about 30 logo ideas on paper, showed the owner three, and refined the one he chose.

Result or learning

The owner now uses the menu board and cup sleeves in the shop. I learned how to design for print at very small sizes, and how to make Tamil and English text feel equal instead of one looking like an afterthought.

My role: Designer and illustrator December Music Season

Margazhi Season poster series

What I did

Made a series of three posters for the December music season, one each for veena, nadaswaram and mridangam. I hand-drew each instrument in Procreate and paired it with a bold Tamil headline that I lettered myself.

Result or learning

I learned how to keep a series consistent with one grid, one colour rule and one type pair, while still letting each poster feel different.
